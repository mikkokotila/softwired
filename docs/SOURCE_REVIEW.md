The OCR has spelling, column-order and numeric errors. It is not a certified transcription, and diagrams/statistical values have not all been manually verified.

The app uses newly authored explanations. It does not quote the original or pretend that AI has directly read the scan. The page references identify the syllabus location, not exact quotations. The AI receives the relevant app lesson from the server. There is no vision/PDF upload feature or public source-page viewer; these are optional features not implemented.

## Material source corrections and choices

1. **Nothing in an unconscious person's mouth.** PDF 58/book 121 suggests honey by mouth for unconscious hypoglycaemia. Visually confirmed. Rejected throughout lesson 8.3-4 and its questions. Oral sugar requires an alert person who can swallow safely. Otherwise 112, airway/breathing assessment and appropriate first aid. [SPR low blood sugar](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/diabeetikon-verensokerin-lasku/).
2. **Gasping is not normal breathing.** The old text around PDF 50–51 may lead a learner to delay CPR while turning/checking repeatedly. App teaching uses immediate 112 after unresponsiveness, a brief normal-breathing check and CPR if breathing is absent/abnormal or uncertain. No blind finger sweeps, pain testing or waiting for a pulse. [SPR adult CPR](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/elvytys/aikuisen-elvytys-painelu-puhalluselvytys/).
3. **Current lay CPR and AED distinctions.** The [15 October 2026 Käypä hoito guideline](https://www.kaypahoito.fi/hoi17010) and [summary](https://www.kaypahoito.fi/kht00112) were opened in the final cross-check. Adult breaths/30:2 require training and competence; otherwise continuous compression is taught. For a rescuer without child-CPR training, the dispatcher guides five initial breaths then 30:2; trained paediatric professionals use 15:2. Failed/impossible breaths must not delay compression. Adult 100–120/min and 5–6 cm; child depth about one-third chest and 100–120/min. Drowning has five initial breaths. No touching during AED rhythm analysis/shock. [SPR child CPR](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/elvytys/lapsen-elvytys/), [SPR AED](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/elvytys/aikuisen-elvytys-defibrillaattorin-avulla/). Infant-specific technique and professional protocols are not taught as interchangeable with lay child CPR.
4. **Hypothermia threshold.** PDF 48 and glossary use below 36 °C. The lesson uses the usual below-35 °C definition and flags the discrepancy. Symptoms and urgent action take precedence over measuring temperature. [SPR hypothermia](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/hypotermia-alilampoisyys/).
5. **Reversed fat names in glossary.** Visually confirmed on PDF 65/book 128: the saturated/unsaturated labels conflict with the chapter explanation. The course consistently teaches saturated as usually hard and unsaturated as usually soft, allowing exceptions. It does not copy the reversed glossary definitions.
6. **Historical plots are historical.** PDF 25 series end in 2014. No source prevalence percentage is presented as a 2026 statistic, and no numeric values were inferred from unreliable OCR. Source interpretation and percentage-point arithmetic are taught with labelled invented examples.
7. **Old legal details are not current law.** The book conflates juridical parenthood and guardianship and includes old age-based confidentiality rules, drug procedures and penalties. The app distinguishes relationships conceptually and omits outdated legal specifics rather than pretending to provide current individual legal advice. A teacher should review the terminology expected on this particular exam.
8. **Non-stigmatising, precise health language.** The course describes people with addiction rather than using the book's stigmatising labels. Reward learning is not reduced to a simple happiness chemical. Common distress is not diagnosed automatically. Family diversity, consent and boundaries are taught without requesting sensitive disclosures.
9. **Nutrition and movement nuance.** The course avoids absolutes such as zero blood-sugar effect from a food, one universal posture, guaranteed disease outcomes or natural/plant origin equalling safety. It teaches dietary patterns, adequate energy for growth, B12 planning and appropriate professional support. Exact supplement dosing and weight-loss coaching are not provided.

Other current first-aid checks: [FAST](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/aivoverenkierron-hairiot/), [asthma](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/astmakohtaus/), [seizures](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/kouristelu/), [bleeding](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/verenvuodon-tyrehdyttaminen/), [shock](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/sokki-verenkierron-hairiotila/), [emergency call](https://www.punainenristi.fi/ensiapu/ensiapuohjeet/hatailmoituksen-tekeminen/).
