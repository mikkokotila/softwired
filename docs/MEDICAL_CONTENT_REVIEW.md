Current adult basics from the 2026 Finnish guideline:

- If unresponsive and not breathing normally, suspect cardiac arrest; call 112 immediately, use speakerphone and follow dispatcher.
- Start compressions center of chest, 100–120/min.
- If TRAINED and competent, alternate 30 compressions and 2 breaths; otherwise do continuous chest compressions.
- Follow automated external defibrillator (AED) instructions as soon as available.
- Unconscious adult who IS breathing normally: place in recovery position, maintain airway, monitor, 112.
- Children: 5 initial rescue breaths; if trained and competent, 15 compressions : 2 breaths; lay rescuers may be directed 30:2. If unable to ventilate, start compressions and add breaths when able.
- If in actual emergency: ALWAYS prioritize rescuer safety and 112 instructions.
